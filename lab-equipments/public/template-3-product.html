FAQ about The Full Night In.

Can we play this with friends? +
The Full Night In is designed for couples. Some Talk cards can work in small groups, but Flirt & Dare decks are definitely “just us two” territory.
Is it LGBTQ+ friendly? +
Yes. Prompts are written to work for all genders & pairings. We avoid gendered roles or assumptions.
What if one of us is shy? +
Start with Talk cards only and agree a “skip” rule if something feels too much. Most couples find they naturally get braver as the night goes on.
Is this safe if I have dietary restrictions? +
Our chocolate is vegan & gluten free, but may contain traces of nuts & soy. If you have specific medical conditions or are pregnant, please check with your doctor before consuming botanical ingredients.
