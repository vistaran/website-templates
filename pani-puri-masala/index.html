👨‍🍳 HOW TO USE

4 Steps to
Golgappa Heaven

1

Mix the Masala

Add 1 tbsp of चटपटा KING to 500ml chilled water. Stir until the water turns a fiery orange-red.

2a

Classic Stuff

Fill a crisp golgappa with boiled potato & chickpeas, dunk in spiced water for 2 seconds — the OG way.

2b

Bomb Style

Skip the potato! Fill with sprouted moong, pomegranate & a dash of sweet chutney for a crunchy, tangy twist.

3

Pop & Repeat

Pop the whole thing in your mouth. Feel the crunch, the tang, the heat. Repeat until the bowl is empty.
